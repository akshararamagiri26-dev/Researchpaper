# Preliminary novelty assessment (scoping review, not systematic review)

**Review date:** 23 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Bound the novelty claim for the manuscript *A Knowledge-Guided AI Decision System for Data-Driven Digital Transformation and Organizational Performance Optimization*.  
**Scope limitation:** This is a preliminary web-based scoping review of selected relevant sources. It is not a systematic review, does not claim exhaustive Scopus/Web of Science coverage, and cannot establish absence of prior art.

## Search approach used for this preliminary assessment

Search phrases included:
- "knowledge-guided decision support hybrid knowledge machine learning ranking digital transformation initiatives"
- "knowledge-based systems hybrid machine learning decision support systems systematic review"
- "prescriptive analytics systems systematic literature review information systems"
- "intelligent decision support systems literature framework machine learning"
- "machine learning hybrid intelligence production management systematic review"

Search results and publisher/metadata pages were inspected for recent reviews/frameworks directly relevant to prescriptive analytics, intelligent decision support, and hybrid intelligence. A formal submission-grade review should repeat and document searches in Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore, and relevant publisher indexes, record exact dates and search strings, deduplicate results, define inclusion/exclusion criteria, and screen titles/abstracts/full texts.

## Defensible current contribution statement

The manuscript currently contributes a **reproducible demonstration protocol** that makes explicit (i) an interpretable weighted knowledge score, (ii) a supervised predictor, (iii) validation-only selection of a mixture weight, and (iv) held-out top-k ranking metrics in a digital-transformation prioritization example. This is a scope statement, not proof of research novelty. The synthetic outcome equation overlaps with the knowledge score, so the current benchmark is not an independent test of the knowledge rules and cannot support organizational-effectiveness claims.

## What is required before asserting novelty

1. Complete a documented database search and screen directly related papers.
2. Build a comparison matrix covering domain, data, knowledge representation, integration mechanism, weight selection, ranking metrics, validation protocol, human oversight, and empirical validation.
3. Identify a specific capability or evaluation gap not already addressed by the closest studies.
4. If no meaningful technical difference remains, position the work as a software/reproducibility note only if the journal's article scope supports that contribution, or redesign the study.
5. Do not describe this preliminary assessment as a systematic literature review and do not claim the proposed hybrid is the first of its kind.
